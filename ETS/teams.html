Bridge Building Team

For 9th-12th ETS Students

Using materials supplied by SAEOPP, students are tasked with designing and constructing a model bridge using the construction guidelines provided. Each team may consist of up to 6 students and one supervisor. The bridge must be built at the competition within a 3-hour time limit.

High-Q Academic Team

For 9th-12th ETS Students

High-Q is a quick recall team competition for Educational Talent Search High School students. Each competition consists of a round robin format with multiple rounds. Each team consists of up to six students with four students playing in a round.

Academic Bowl

For 7th-8th ETS Students

Academic Bowl is a series of competitions for Educational Talent Search Middle School students. An Academic Bowl team can consist of between 6-12 students. The competitions held are a Spelling Bee, Written Exam, and Super Quiz. Both individual and team awards will be given from each category and the overall winner will be determined by a team score from the three events.

About Us

ETS (Educational talent search) is a program for 6th graders to 12th graders to help them prepare for college and give them all the tools they need to succeed. ETS (Educational talent search) is a program for 6th graders to 12th graders to help them prepare for college and careers and give them all the tools they need to succeed. Through free monthly in-school workshops with activities to develop college readiness skills and to identify and pursue career pathways. Free events for students and family covering topics like SAT Prep, financial aid, and leadership skills. Free trips to tour career and technical colleges, community colleges, and public or private institutions. Along with visiting work sites for high-demand careers and trades. During these trips, students will also participate in fun activities offered by businesses and organizations local to the campus/site.

Educational Talent Search is one of eight federal TRIO Programs funded by the US Department of Education. We serve a minimum of 680 students attending target schools in Clark and Fayette Counties. ETS also serves adults desiring to re-enter and complete their education by attaining a high school diploma or GED and pursuing postsecondary education.
