What is ETS?

ETS (Educational talent search) is a program for 6th graders to 12th graders to help them prepare for college and give them all the tools they need to succeed.

Educational Talent Search is one of eight federal TRIO Programs funded by the US Department of Education. We serve a minimum of 680 students attending target schools in Clark, Fayette, and Scott Counties. ETS also serves adults desiring to re-enter and complete their education by attaining a high school diploma or GED and pursuing postsecondary education.

What does ETS offer?

  • Free monthly in-school workshops with themes to develop college readiness and social and emotional skills.
  • Free events for students and family covering topics like ACT Prep, financial aid, and summer camps.
  • Free trips to tour college and experience cultural enrichment activities around the country.
